∇ Nabla

Symbolic math, typeset properly. Nothing leaves the device.

  • d/dx derivatives to any order
  • ∫ indefinite and definite, oo allowed in bounds
  • lim one‑ or two‑sided
  • solve type x^2 = 4 or just x^2 - 4
  • plot comma‑separate up to four functions

Type loosely — 2x, x^2, sin x all parse. Constants: pi, e, oo. ln is the natural log, log is base 10 (log(x, 2) for any other base).
